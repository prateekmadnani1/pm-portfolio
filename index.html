    Who is Prateek Madnani?

    Prateek Madnani is a senior technical product manager in Seattle. He started as a software engineer in Bengaluru, building Java services and cloud microservices at Checkpoint Systems and Blackhawk Network, then moved into product, owning AI and platform products at the American Arbitration Association and Dentite. Since July 2026 he leads platform modernization at AgingOptions.

    6+ years building and shipping software across legal tech, fintech, healthcare, retail and travel technology.

      What kind of products does Prateek build?

      AI products and cloud platforms, mostly in regulated industries. At the American Arbitration Association he was the technical product manager for a multi-agent GenAI platform that evaluates legal claims. At Dentite he defined the roadmap for AI-assisted dental insurance claims. At AgingOptions he is moving a legacy legal-tech platform to the cloud.

      What did he do before product management?

      He was a software engineer. At Checkpoint Systems he built an RFID location service on Azure that processes more than 10 million events a day. At Blackhawk Network he built developer platform services for global payment systems, using Java, Spring Boot and AWS.

      How does he work with engineering teams?

      As someone who has been on that side. He can sit in an architecture review in the morning and an executive roadmap meeting in the afternoon, and translate between the two. At AgingOptions he treated internal developer tooling as a product, and that work raised release velocity by 10%.

      What has he written or published?

      A free guide, The Technical Product Manager Handbook, a podcast called The Pro Product Manager, a research paper on AI and climate action that he presented at the United Nations, and short essays on LinkedIn.

      What is he certified in?

      The NextLeap Product Manager Fellowship (2025), Google Project Management Professional (2024), Google AI Essentials (2024) and Generative AI for Product Managers from LinkedIn (2024). He also passed LinkedIn's Java skill assessment.

      How can I contact him?

      Email is best: prateekmadnani01@gmail.com. He is also on LinkedIn, and glad to talk about AI products, platform modernization, or moving from engineering into product.
